Rudnya packaging plant brings a new production line into service

A new line forms part of the Rudnya plant's expansion. The reported opening is separate from two further lines still being installed and from customer delivery commitments.

2026-05-20

Rudnya's packaging plant has brought a new production line into service as part of an expansion programme. This company update distinguishes the announced opening from equipment still being installed and from the terms of future customer orders.

A new line within the wider project

Interfax reported on May 20, 2026, citing Smolensk regional governor Vasily Anokhin, that investment in the project exceeded one billion roubles. Output was reported at up to 40 million units a month, while two further production lines remained under installation.

The scope of the production figure

The monthly figure describes the reported production scale at the plant; it is not a promise to deliver that quantity to any particular buyer. Multiplying it by twelve would create an annualised calculation, not an observed annual result. The announcement does not establish an individual customer's allocation, purchase price or delivery date.

Separate stages in the expansion

  • The commissioned line is the completed development described in the report.
  • Installation of further equipment is a separate work stage, not confirmation that every planned line is operating.
  • A customer's confirmed supply programme requires its own specification and commercial agreement.

These distinctions keep the industrial announcement separate from a sales offer. A request for packaging needs to identify the required container and quantity rather than rely on the plant-wide total. Production scale alone does not define the exact packaging that will be supplied or demonstrate compatibility with a buyer's filling process.

Customer enquiries and company information

The opening is relevant to the plant's development, but the report is not a substitute for checking the product specification and order terms with the supplier. No later completion of the additional lines is assumed in this account. The status recorded here remains the status described in the dated announcement.

Company: Rudnyansky Tarny Kombinat LLC, Rudnya, Smolensk region. Reference contacts listed for the plant in the municipal enterprise directory: 5-15-66 and 5-15-80, in local Rudnya telephone format. This is a separate directory contact block, not contact information quoted from the May announcement.

Corporate information about the plant is published on the Kaliningrad Packaging Plant website: https://tarkom.ru/. The two plant names and their contact details should not be treated as interchangeable.
