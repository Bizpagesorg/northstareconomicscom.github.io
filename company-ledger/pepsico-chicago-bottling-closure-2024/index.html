PepsiCo announces Chicago bottling plant closure

PepsiCo explained its Chicago bottling closure while the union challenged the notification process.

2024-11-06

PepsiCo attributed its Chicago bottling plant closure to physical limitations at the site, Manufacturing Dive reported on 6 November 2024. Teamsters Local 727 objected to the notification process.

Chicago closure and the wider bottling network

Associated Press reported on 30 October that Chicago was the only complete site closure among four bottling locations covered by the company's announcement. Sales, delivery and warehouse operations were to continue at Cincinnati, Harrisburg and Atlanta. The distinction separates the end of bottling activity from the removal of every function at a location.

Company and union statements

The company said it would work with union leadership and maintained that its plans met applicable requirements. The union disputed the notice process; no legal determination is reported here.

In its 28 October report, Associated Press said PepsiCo would continue paying affected workers for 60 days without requiring them to work. The union said that summer negotiations for a new contract had not disclosed a possible closure.

About PepsiCo

PepsiCo's corporate description published on 30 August 2024 identified a business spanning beverages and convenient foods, with products reaching more than 200 countries and territories. It reported net revenue above $91 billion for 2023. Those figures describe the company as a whole, rather than production or revenue at the Chicago facility.

The portfolio named in that historical company release included:

  • Pepsi-Cola, Mountain Dew and Gatorade;
  • Lay's, Doritos and Cheetos;
  • Quaker and SodaStream.
