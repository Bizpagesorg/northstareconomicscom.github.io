Schneider Electric adds low-voltage production to its Dunavecse expansion

Schneider Electric announces a €44 million expansion in Dunavecse, introducing low-voltage distribution equipment at the Hungarian site.

2025-03-07

Schneider Electric has announced a €44 million expansion of its Dunavecse manufacturing site in Hungary. The planned project would introduce low-voltage distribution equipment to the location through an 18,000-square-metre facility.

Australian Manufacturing reported the expansion on 7 March 2025, in an article by Kate B. based on the company announcement. The report is coverage of the declared project, not independent verification of its eventual operating results.

A new product scope at an existing site

The company's 4 March 2025 announcement concerns an extension of Duna Smart Power Systems, which opened in 2024. The new production activity should be distinguished from the site's original opening: it changes the range of equipment to be manufactured there, rather than simply restating the earlier inauguration.

The announcement is available from the company's newsroom: https://www.se.com/ww/en/about-us/newsroom/news/press-releases/schneider-electric-implements-another-44-million-investment-in-dunavecse-67c69ee172f0cd1c740bdbe3/.

Reading the development milestones

Separate the measures

The investment amount and floor area describe the scope of the development. Neither figure is a count of finished equipment delivered to customers. When following the project, readers should keep the construction programme, introduction of the product range and subsequent operating results separate. A report of one milestone does not automatically establish the others.

  • Project scope: distinguish the building programme from the equipment installed inside it.
  • Product scope: establish which products a later operating update actually covers.
  • Operating evidence: future reporting would be needed to establish actual production and deliveries.

For customers and project partners, the practical questions concern product availability, the applicable specifications and delivery arrangements. Those details should be confirmed through the company's relevant commercial channels rather than inferred from the scale of a building investment. This account does not supply a commissioning schedule, a production-volume target or a guarantee of order availability.

The development is recorded here as announced on 4 March 2025. Subsequent construction progress or changes to the programme would require separate dated confirmation. Keeping that boundary clear allows readers to follow the expansion without treating an investment announcement as a completed operating result.
