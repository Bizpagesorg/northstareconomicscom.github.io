Omsk manufacturer expands cryogenic valve capacity

Kriogennaya Tekhnika expands Omsk valve capacity with RUB135 million of investment. Its annual production target remains distinct from completed deliveries.

2026-06-17

NTK Kriogennaya Tekhnika has modernised cryogenic valve production in Omsk, doubling manufacturing capacity, Interfax reported on June 17, citing the Industrial Development Fund. Investment totals RUB 135 million, including a RUB 100 million fund loan. The project concerns pipeline fittings, not the company's earlier vacuum-pump investment.

Expanded capacity and the production target

The company plans to reach up to 650 units annually after the operation reaches its design capacity. That figure is a target for the expanded operation, not a count of units already delivered. The announcement should therefore be read as an update on manufacturing capability and a planned production level.

A capacity expansion and a customer's delivery date are different commercial measures. Available machinery creates an opportunity to schedule more work, but a particular order still requires an agreed specification, quantity and completion date. The annual target does not establish an immediately available stock of any particular fitting.

Product definitions remain important

The company's official catalogue distinguishes several categories of valves and related equipment. For procurement, that distinction matters more than a headline total alone. A buyer needs the appropriate product documentation rather than assuming that every item in the range performs the same role.

  • Identify the exact product designation in the commercial enquiry.
  • Keep the requested quantity separate from the supplier's annual capacity.
  • Agree which documentation accompanies the supplied item.
  • Confirm the delivery schedule for the specific order.

These points concern the organisation of a purchase, not technical instructions for selecting, installing or operating cryogenic equipment. The production announcement does not establish suitability for an individual installation. Such a decision depends on the applicable project documentation and qualified technical assessment.

Investment and operating results

The financing figure describes resources committed to the modernisation. It is not a sales total, and the loan is part of that investment rather than an additional amount to place on top of it. Subsequent production and deliveries would show how the expanded operation is being used. Until those results are available, the announced capacity and the output objective should retain their separate meanings.
